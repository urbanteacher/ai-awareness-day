Safe Did you know?

young people have been targeted by deepfake image abuse.
Sources cited in our lesson slides: Thorn Research 2025, European Parliament 2025, European Commission.
Safe Three habits
Staying safe in an AI world
01
StopBefore sharing, ask: could this be AI-generated?
02
VerifyCheck the source, search the image, or ask a trusted adult.
03
ReportAI-generated intimate images of anyone are illegal. Tell a trusted adult.
