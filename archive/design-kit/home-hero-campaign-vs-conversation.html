Homepage hero: telling the Day and the Conversation apart

Today the hero sells both at once: one headline, one date, and two buttons side by side ("Get involved with AIAD27" and "Join the National Conversation"), so a visitor can't tell they are two different things with two different dates. Each mock below gives each offer its own colour, its own date and its own button.

How it works

Whichever we pick

  • One colour per offer, used everywhere: purple for the Day, blue for the Conversation (the National Conversation page is already blue).
  • Every button names one offer. Today "Get involved with AIAD27" and "Join the National Conversation" sit side by side as if they were the same kind of thing.
  • Each offer gets one date it owns: 29 April for the Day; "opens 1 January" for the Conversation.
  • The countdown belongs to the Day only.
