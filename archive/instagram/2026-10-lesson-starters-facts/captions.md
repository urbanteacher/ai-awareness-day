# Lesson starters, version 2: a fact and three habits for each theme (Instagram carousel, 18 slides)

`slide-01.png` to `slide-18.png`, each 1080×1350 (4:5). Made from `archive/design-kit/ig-resources-facts-slides.html`
(`PW=<playwright dir> node render-benchmark-slides.mjs <this folder> ig-resources-facts-slides.html`). The first version
(the big questions) is kept in `archive/instagram/2026-10-lesson-starters/`.

This version uses the content of our own lesson slide decks (`assets/lessons/2027/*.pdf`): the "Did you know?" slide, the
"three habits" slide and the key takeaway for the five starters that have a deck, one per theme. Each theme's icon is the
backdrop. "AI Relationships?" is left out because it has no deck and its two figures (20% and 58%) have no source on its page.

1. Cover: five lesson starters, five themes
2. Safe, overview: Who's really behind the screen? and its big question
3. Safe, did you know: 1 in 4 UK teenagers (26% of 13 to 18 year olds) have seen a sexualised deepfake (Girlguiding, Feb 2025)
4. Safe, three habits (Stop, Verify, Report). Takeaway: Think before you post.
5. Smart, overview: How does AI actually 'think'?
6. Smart, did you know: 88% of UK undergraduates use generative AI for assessments, up from 53% (HEPI/Kortext survey of 1,041 students, Feb 2025)
7. Smart, three habits (Know what it is, Verify, Be specific). Takeaway: Autocomplete, not an oracle.
8. Creative, overview: AI as your creative partner
9. Creative, did you know: 23% of UK creative employers report skills gaps caused by new technology (Creative Industries Policy and Evidence Centre, Skills Audits 2026)
10. Creative, three habits (Brainstorm with it, Bring yourself, Start do not finish). Takeaway: You are still the author.
11. Responsible, overview: The hidden costs of AI
12. Responsible, did you know: data centres use 2.5% of Britain's electricity, forecast to quadruple by 2030 (NESO, via the Environmental Audit Committee)
13. Responsible, three habits (Think first, Check yourself first, Ask for transparency). Takeaway: Thoughtfully, not never.
14. Future, overview: Your AI-ready future
15. Future, did you know: 95,000 AI-related roles created in the UK since 2023 (LinkedIn, reported by PA, June 2026)
16. Future, three habits (Lead with human skills, Learn to work with AI, Stay adaptable). Takeaway: Collaborate, and stay human.
17. Slides and a plan, ready to go
18. Try one tomorrow (link in bio)

Each overview slide is in the theme's colour with its icon as the backdrop; the title, subtitle and big question are from the
cover and second slide of that lesson's deck.

All five "did you know" figures are UK data. They replace the figures in our lesson decks, which are mostly global or
US (Thorn, WEF, an NYC comparison). Each was checked on the source or in coverage of it on 30 October 2026:
- Girlguiding's press release was blocked to me, so the 26% comes from its coverage; check the release before quoting.
- The HEPI page gives 88% for assessments; the country is not stated on the page but HEPI's survey is of UK undergraduates.
- 2.5% and "quadruple by 2030" come from parliamentary evidence citing NESO; the House of Commons Library briefing was blocked to me.
- 95,000 is LinkedIn's figure reported by PA (via LBC); the 23% is "skills gaps resulting from new technology", not AI alone.
The three habits and takeaways are unchanged, from the decks.
