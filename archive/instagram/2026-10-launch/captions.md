AI Awareness Day 2027 has a new date and a new theme. 📅

🗓️ Thursday 29 April 2027. Earlier in the year, away from exam season, so the whole school can take part.

🔁 Our theme is Humans in the Loop: young people questioning AI, debating it and deciding the role it should play in their lives.

From 1 January, schools can join the National AI Conversation, with classroom discussions, school debates and a National Debate Network across five themes: Safe, Smart, Creative, Responsible and Future.

## 2. Safe (02-theme-safe)

Should AI remember everything you tell it? 🛡️

It's one of five big questions in the National AI Conversation. Try it as a 5-minute starter this week: ask the class, take a vote, then ask who changed their mind and why.

Theme: SAFE. Privacy, trust and what AI knows about us.

#AIAwarenessDay #KeepHumansInTheLoop #AIinEducation #OnlineSafety #Oracy

## 3. Smart (03-theme-smart)

Should students be allowed to use AI in assessed work? 💡

Every school is wrestling with this one. Put it to your students and hear what they actually think.

Theme: SMART. Learning, thinking for yourself and knowing when to check.

#AIAwarenessDay #KeepHumansInTheLoop #AIinEducation #Assessment #CriticalThinking

## 4. Creative (04-theme-creative)

If you make something with AI, is it still your work? ✨

A great debate for art, music, English and computing classrooms.

Theme: CREATIVE. Authorship, originality and imagination.

#AIAwarenessDay #KeepHumansInTheLoop #AIinEducation #Creativity #DigitalArt

## 5. Responsible (05-theme-responsible)

Who should be responsible when AI gets something wrong? ⚖️

The company? The user? The person who trusted it? Let your class argue it out.

Theme: RESPONSIBLE. Accountability, fairness and consequences.
