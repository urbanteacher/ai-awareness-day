# A peek inside the AI audit (Instagram carousel, 15 slides)

`slide-01.png` to `slide-15.png`, each 1080×1350 (4:5), posted in order. Made from `archive/design-kit/ig-benchmark-slides.html`
(`PW=<playwright dir> node render-benchmark-slides.mjs <this folder>`). The first, denser version (8 slides) is kept in
`archive/instagram/2026-10-ai-audit-carousel-v1-dense/`.

This version follows what carousel guides and Canva's own advice say works on a phone: one idea per slide, a big hook on
slide 1, headlines around 100px or more and body text around 40px or more at 1080 wide, text kept off the edges, and a
check at about a third of the size (the contact-sheet test). Everything is drawn, not screenshotted. The questions are
samples (the real tool has 14 to 22 per role); the results (62 / 100, Moderate, 38%, and the school bars) are made up and
each is marked "Sample".
