# 20 big questions for the National AI Conversation

Four questions for each theme, taken from the National AI Conversation motion bank:
one for all ages, then primary, secondary and post-16 (a debate motion).
Post them in file-number order so the colours rotate through the five themes. Post one or two a week
from now until AI Awareness Day on Thursday 29 April 2027.

All images are 1080×1350 and carry no dates, so they also work on other platforms.

---

## 01. Safe, All ages (big-question-01-safe.png)

🛡️ Big question.

Should AI remember everything you tell it?

Try it as a 5-minute starter: ask the class, take a vote, then ask who changed their mind and why.

Part of the National AI Conversation. Theme: SAFE.
