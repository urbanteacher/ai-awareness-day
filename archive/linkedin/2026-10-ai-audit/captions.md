# The AI Risk & Readiness Benchmark: free AI audit (LinkedIn)

One square image, 1200×1200: `ai-audit-1200x1200.png`. The Instagram portrait (1080×1350) is in
`archive/instagram/2026-10-ai-audit/`. Both come from `archive/design-kit/ig-benchmark.html`
(`PW=<playwright dir> node render-benchmark-cards.mjs <linkedin folder> <instagram folder>`). The certificate is a sample
with a generic name ("Your Name"); the real ones carry the person's name, a certificate ID and a web address.

The tool: aiawarenessday.co.uk/timeline/ai-risk-readiness-benchmark/. It asks 14 to 22 questions depending on the role
(teacher 22, student 14, parent 15, leader 16, general public 18) and describes itself as a 10 to 15 minute audit, so the
cards say "10–15 min", not "10 questions".

## LinkedIn post

How much do you rely on AI? Do you check what it tells you? Could you manage without it?

Most AI audits ask whether a school has a policy. Few ask what people actually do. So we built a free one that does.

The AI Risk & Readiness Benchmark takes 10–15 minutes and it's anonymous. There's a version for:
👩‍🏫 Teachers: reliance, verification and the data you put into AI tools
🎓 Students: critical thinking and whether you could do the work without it
👪 Parents: what your child does with AI at home, and the risks
🏫 School leaders: governance, policy and safeguarding

You get your readiness score and tailored next steps, and schools can build a picture across their whole community. No student personal data is collected.

Take it yourself, then share it with a colleague, a class or the parents' group:
👉 aiawarenessday.co.uk/timeline/ai-risk-readiness-benchmark/

Keep Humans in the Loop starts with knowing where you are.
