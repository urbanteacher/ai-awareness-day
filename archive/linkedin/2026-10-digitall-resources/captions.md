## Post

New on the AI Awareness Day website: two more classroom resources from our event partner DigitALL. 🎉

💡 Spark AI: inclusive and alternative AI for teachers and pupils, with free classroom resources and teacher training. digitall.charity/sparkai
🌍 EcoCode: free micro:bit activity packs that teach coding and AI through sustainability. digitall.charity/ecocode

You'll find both in Extra Resources on our homepage, alongside a handpicked set of AI games and learning tools from trusted organisations: aiawarenessday.co.uk

A big thank you to Peter Marshman and the DigitALL team for sharing them with schools. Helping young people become confident, discerning users and developers of technology is Humans in the Loop in action.

AI Awareness Day is on Thursday 29 April 2027.
