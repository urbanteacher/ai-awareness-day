## Post

Is the human the new bottleneck?

Human in the loop? Why, if they are slowing things up checking everything?

Anthropic's latest report this week shows what is on the horizon. Inside the company, AI sessions spend roughly 80% of their time waiting for a human.

What else it found:
📉 The junior share of technical hires fell from 27% to 9%
⚙️ Agents logged six hours of activity for every hour of human work
👀 By one estimate, reading all the agent output would take more than half a working week
🔮 Staff expect AI to be able to do 75% of their current work hours by 2030

And in schools? If teachers use AI to create work, mark work and analyse data, and it does some of that better than they can, is that efficiency, or outsourcing their effort? If pupils paste in answers they cannot explain, what are they learning? And what career advice do we give young people when entry-level hiring is falling?

This is why Keep Humans in the Loop is the theme of AI Awareness Day 2027. The people who direct, check and decide need judgement, and judgement has to be learned. From 1 January 2027, the National AI Conversation invites primary, secondary and post-16 students to question, discuss and debate the role AI should play in their lives and futures, building up to AI Awareness Day on Thursday 29 April 2027.
