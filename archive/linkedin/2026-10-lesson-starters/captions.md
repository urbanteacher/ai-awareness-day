## Post

Five minutes, one big question, and a class that's talking.

We've made six free lesson starters on AI, and here's a taster of each. From "Who do you think is the real creator, you or the AI?" to "If AI can do many jobs faster than humans, what skills will make you valuable?"

How a starter works: ask the big question, pairs talk for 60 seconds, vote, look at the facts, and end on one takeaway. There are slides and a printable lesson plan, and most have a debate for primary, secondary and post-16.

They're built for oracy: structured talk, listening and changing your mind, which is what the National AI Conversation opens up in January 2027.
