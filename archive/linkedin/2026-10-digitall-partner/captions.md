## Post

We're excited to announce that DigitALL has joined the AI Awareness Day campaign as an event partner. 🎉

DigitALL is a charity that equips young people in schools and their local communities with the digital skills and leadership they need for the digital careers of today and tomorrow, learning through climate and sustainability.

Two places to start, both free to explore:
💡 Spark AI: inclusive and alternative AI for teachers and pupils. digitall.charity/sparkai
🌍 EcoCode: coding and computational thinking through sustainability, with the micro:bit. digitall.charity/ecocode

That's our 2027 theme, Humans in the Loop, in practice: young people who are confident, discerning users and developers of technology.

AI Awareness Day is on Thursday 29 April 2027. aiawarenessday.co.uk

#AIAwarenessDay #AILiteracy #AIinEducation #KeepHumansInTheLoop #DigitalSkills
