## Post

We're delighted to welcome AILitKit as an event partner for AI Awareness Day 2027. 🎉

AILitKit helps teachers bring AI literacy into the lessons they already teach, in any subject and any year group. Describe a lesson and it gives you a guide with four classroom activities, unplugged, plugged, discussion and creative, each with what to say and questions to ask.

What we love: every guide includes an unplugged activity that needs no devices, and pupils never need an account. That fits our 2027 theme, Humans in the Loop, perfectly. It's about helping young people question AI, not just use it.

Free to explore, no sign-in needed: where AI literacy fits in English, RE, Geography, Maths, PE, Music and Science, with sample guides from Year 1 to Year 11.
👉 ailitkit.com/ai-literacy

Thank you to Matthew Wemyss and the IN&ED team for joining us.

From 1 January 2027, schools can join the National AI Conversation, building to AI Awareness Day on Thursday 29 April 2027.
👉 aiawarenessday.co.uk
