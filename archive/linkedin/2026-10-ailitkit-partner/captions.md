## Post

We're excited to announce that AILitKit has joined the AI Awareness Day campaign as an event partner. 🎉

AILitKit helps teachers bring AI literacy into the lessons they already teach, in any subject and year group. Describe a lesson and it gives you a guide with four classroom activities: unplugged, plugged, discussion and creative.

Every guide includes an unplugged activity that needs no devices, and pupils never need an account. That fits our 2027 theme, Humans in the Loop: helping young people question AI, not just use it.

Free to explore, no sign-in needed: ailitkit.com/ai-literacy

Thank you to Matthew Wemyss and the IN&ED team for joining us.

AI Awareness Day is on Thursday 29 April 2027. aiawarenessday.co.uk
